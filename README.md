This is a repository for the documentation, bios, cards, and history of *All Star Wrestling Federation*, my personal
e-fed for close to twenty years. Currently I'm working on rebirthing it as a game on [Pro Wrestling Sim](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1157700/Pro_Wrestling_Sim/).

# All Star Wrestling Federation: Tradition Rules

*All Star Wrestling Federation* is a hybrid wrestling organisation that aims to strike a balance between "old-school"
and “new-school”.

## Quick Statistics

- Founded: 1991
- Headquarters: Toronto, Ontario
- Website: [Official](https://www.aswfwrestling.com)

## Programs

- **[Turbulance](http://aswf.integraldata.cc/shows/turbulance)**: Flagship program, airs Tuesday at 21:00. Hosted by
  Dan Crawford, Rex Roseman, and J. Clear
- **[Thrasher!](http://aswf.integraldata.cc/shows/thrasher)**: Secondary program, airs Friday at 22:00. Hosted by J.
  Clear and Rikky Bull. Aims to be a throwback to the wrestling shows of the 70's and 80's.
- **[In Your Face!](http://aswf.integraldata.cc/shows/inyourface)**: Wrap up and review, airs online several times.
  Hosted by Rex Roseman
- **[ASWF\.com Radio](http://aswf.integraldata.cc/radio)**: Updates throughout the day (and wrap up on Sunday). On
  demand through [ASWF.com](http://aswf.integraldata.cc). Hosted by Ken Steeves. Originally a flash packet content on
  RSPWF for weekly flash roleplays.

## Events

### Agony & Ecstasy

The one that kicks off the new year of ASWF. Typically occurs around New Years, in the first or second week of January
(though occaisionally will happen in the final weeks of December). Features a multi-ring battle royal with 40-60
competitors. The rules for the match are, as follows:

**Rules:**

- Anyone who wishes a shot at the championship is free to enter, though the order of entry is weighted by win-loss record.
- Typical battle royal rules apply until there are six participants left when the rules change slightly.
- The final six are automatically entered into a series of matches between Agony and Ecstasy and Wrestlestorm to
  determine who faces the champion.
- The first of the six eliminated will need to face the others in order to move on to Wrestlestorm. The next eliminated
  determine the order of the matches.
- Any time someone wins the match, they move on to the next opponent that was eliminated.
- The last man standing at the end of the gauntlet goes on to face the champion.

### **Wrestlestorm [Roman Numeral]**

The flagship event of ASWF and the one that is the most revered. This is usually where mega stories are finished and
new ones begin. Typically occurs in the spring of each year, following Agony & Ecstasy but before World Bash.

### **World Bash: [Slogan related to location, ie: “London Calling]**

An event and corresponding international tour that happens during the summer, following WrestleStorm. The World Bash
event will usually have an internationally significant tagline related to the location they're in. The actual event is
bookended with a tour for the month when all events and house shows take place around the country World Bash takes
place in.

### **Total War**

Happens in the fall (September-November), typically. More hardcore and violent than the usual ASWF events where the
laws and rules of civility and ring are disregarded in favour of violent and brutal rivalries. Specialty match
typically takes place called "Total War".

#### Rules

- Traditionally a cage match, with special rules.
- Made up of two or more teams.
- Teams fight for advantage in the weeks leading up to the match; this determines order.

### **Night of Champions [Roman Numeral]**

Happens throughout the year at various times, typically either as special episodes of regular television or through
separate televised events which are one-off. These are typically free-to-air.
