# "Cold Kick" Dave Martin

"Cold Kick" Dave Martin is the original partner of "Iron Fist" James Paxton. Between the two of them, Dave Martin
preferred a more technical approach to wrestling whereas James Paxton preferred a powerhouse approach.

Although they went their separate ways prior to James Paxton founding The Corporation, he still is actively involved in
the business and within ASWF. What's next for "Cold Kick"? Only he knows.
