# All Star Wrestling Federation: Tradition Rules

*All Star Wrestling Federation* is a hybrid wrestling organisation that aims to strike a balance between “old-school”
and “new-school”.

## Quick Statistics

- Founded: 1991
- Headquarters: Toronto, Ontario
- Website: [Official](https://www.aswfwrestling.com)

## Programs

- **[Turbulance](http://aswf.integraldata.cc/shows/turbulance)**: Flagship program, airs Tuesday at 21:00. Hosted
by Dan Crawford, Rex Roseman, and J. Clear
- **[Thrasher!](http://aswf.integraldata.cc/shows/thrasher)**: Secondary program, airs Friday at 22:00. Hosted
by J. Clear and Rikky Bull. Aims to be a throwback to the wrestling shows of the 70's and 80's.
- **[In Your Face!](http://aswf.integraldata.cc/shows/inyourface)**: Wrap up and review, airs online several times.
Hosted by Rex Roseman
- **[ASWF\.com Radio](http://aswf.integraldata.cc/radio)**: Updates throughout the day (and wrap up on Sunday).
On-demand through [ASWF.com](http://aswf.integraldata.cc). Hosted by Ken Steeves.

## Roster

### Factions

#### The Corporation

The most powerful stable in ASWF, led by James Paxton. With Scott Fuller, James Sanderson, and Sal E. Graziano,
they are a force to be reckoned with, both in and out of the ring.

These characters and teams form the backbone of ASWF, each bringing their own flavour to the federation’s rich
tapestry of storytelling and in-ring action. The balance between old-school respect and new-school flair creates
a unique environment where every match is more than just a fight; it’s a narrative unfolding before the eyes of
the wrestling world. They are soft-allied with President T.N.T., despite being anti-authoritarian. Above all they
crave an elite lifestyle of wealthy and debauchery.

### Staff & Backstage

**President T.N.T**: The enigmatic “Owner” of ASWF, a heel character who is the financial muscle behind the scenes.
His rare appearances are always make an impact, often altering the course of story lines with a single decree.
His alliance with James Paxton and the Corporation hints at a larger, more sinister agenda.

**Sal E. Graziano**: The Corporation’s cunning “Lawyer,” known for his sharp tongue and sharper legal maneuvers.
His expertise in finding loopholes is legendary, ensuring his clients often escape by the skin of their teeth,
much to the chagrin of fans and foes alike.

**“Frankie” Fabulous**: An Authority Figure with a flair for the dramatic, Frankie’s presence is as grand as his
legacy. A face character, he champions fair play and spectacular matches, often clashing with the Corporation’s
underhanded tactics.

**Dan Crawford** & **Blake Bradley**: The voices of ASWF, these Announcers bring every match to life with their
unique blend of insight and excitement, guiding the audience through the highs and lows of each bout.

**Rex Roseman** & **Rikky Bull**: As Colour Commentators, Rex’s modern takes and Rikky’s nostalgic musings
provide a dynamic contrast that enriches the viewing experience, offering perspectives from both ends of wrestling’s
storied history.

**J. Clear**: The unsung hero Backstage Worker, J. Clear’s efforts ensure that the show runs smoothly, a vital
cog in the ASWF machine.

**Tim Dross**: The Analyst with a plan, Tim breaks down the action with a keen eye, offering predictions and post-match
breakdowns that keep the audience hooked. Has been with the company from the start and involved in professional
wrestling for longer. Manages and runs both the ASWF hotline and ASWF Radio.
